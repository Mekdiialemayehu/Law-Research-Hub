🇪🇺 European Convention on Human Rights

Articles

These are the core Convention rights currently included in the website. You can expand this list later with more articles or protocol rights.

Fundamental Right

Article 2 – Right to Life

Protects the right to life and requires States to take positive steps to protect life in certain situations.

Read Note
Absolute Right

Article 3 – Prohibition of Torture

Protects individuals from torture, inhuman treatment, and degrading treatment or punishment.

Read Note
Absolute Protection

Article 4 – Slavery and Forced Labour

Prohibits slavery, servitude, forced labour, and modern forms of exploitation such as trafficking.

Read Note
Liberty

Article 5 – Right to Liberty and Security

Protects against arbitrary detention and requires lawful detention with proper safeguards.

Read Note
Fair Trial

Article 6 – Right to a Fair Trial

Guarantees fairness, independence, impartiality, reasonable time, and defence rights in court proceedings.

Read Note
Privacy

Article 8 – Private and Family Life

Protects privacy, family life, home, and correspondence against unlawful or disproportionate interference.

Read Note
Belief

Article 9 – Thought, Conscience and Religion

Protects the right to hold beliefs, change beliefs, and manifest religion or belief in appropriate circumstances.

Read Note
Speech

Article 10 – Freedom of Expression

Protects opinions, information, ideas, political speech, journalism, and public debate.

Read Note
Assembly

Article 11 – Peaceful Assembly and Association

Protects peaceful protest, association with others, and the right to form and join trade unions.

Read Note
Equality

Article 14 – Prohibition of Discrimination

Protects against discrimination in the enjoyment of Convention rights.
