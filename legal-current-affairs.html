When Is an Investigation Enough? The European Court of Human Rights Rules on Forced Labour

A.S. v. Latvia and the difficult question of what states must do when allegations of exploitation arise

On 3 September 2026, the European Court of Human Rights delivered its judgment in A.S. v. Latvia. The Court found no violation of Article 4 of the European Convention on Human Rights. But the case raises a difficult question: when someone alleges exploitation, what exactly must a state do to satisfy its human-rights obligations?
