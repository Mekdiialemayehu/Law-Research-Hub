About the Project

Law Research Hub is an educational website created to make legal concepts more accessible to students. Through carefully researched case studies, concise notes, and interactive quizzes, the project aims to help learners understand how courts interpret the law and protect human rights.


The website reflects my interest in criminal law, human rights law, legal reasoning, and academic research while encouraging others to explore landmark court decisions from around the world.
